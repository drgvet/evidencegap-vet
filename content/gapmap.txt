== Myxomatous mitral valve disease (dogs)

question: Pimobendan in preclinical stage B2 disease
species: dog
stage: pre
certainty: moderate
direction: benefit
qualifier: in stage B2 dogs only, on a composite endpoint, in a trial stopped early and never repeated
basis: 1 randomised trial, 360 dogs, placebo-controlled and double-blind, clinical endpoint
sources: EPIC, 2016 · FDA NADA 141-273, 2025 · [full appraisal](review-pimobendan-b2.html)
appraisal: review-pimobendan-b2.html
limitations: Funded by the manufacturer · Stopped early at a pre-planned interim · Nine reclassified dogs remove significance · On proportions alone the result is not significant · Judged by FDA not adequate and well-controlled · Never replicated
verdict:
  EPIC, the single trial of pimobendan in preclinical stage B2 disease, supports delaying heart failure in dogs meeting its entry criteria, and nothing else on this map is better evidenced. It is also more fragile than the headline suggests: nine reclassified dogs remove significance, and on proportions alone the result is not significant. A median difference of 380 days survives the removal of the first 23 weeks, so the benefit cannot be attributed to early failures alone. Whether dogs at the mild end of stage B2 benefit is not answerable from the published data.

question: Does preclinical pimobendan shorten survival once heart failure develops?
species: dog
stage: pre
certainty: low
direction: unclear
qualifier: both cohorts report shorter survival, and confounding by indication explains it equally well
basis: 2 retrospective cohorts, 137 and 143 dogs, no randomisation
sources: McAulay, 2026 · Park, 2025
limitations: Retrospective · Confounding by indication visible in the data · Underpowered
verdict:
  Two retrospective cohorts report shorter survival after heart failure in dogs that had been treated beforehand, and the question they raise is a reasonable one to ask. Neither study is able to answer it, because in both of them the pretreated dogs already had larger hearts at the point at which failure developed, and that difference is sufficient on its own to explain the survival gap. The finding argues for a prospective study, not for a change in practice.

question: Pimobendan in congestive heart failure
species: dog
stage: clin
certainty: moderate
direction: benefit
qualifier: compared with benazepril, which was itself never shown to beat placebo in heart failure
basis: 1 randomised trial, 260 dogs, active comparator, clinical endpoint
sources: QUEST, 2008
appraisal_in_preparation: yes
limitations: Single-blinded, owners not blinded · No placebo arm · Composite endpoint includes investigator-judged treatment failure · Manufacturer-affiliated co-author
verdict:
  The evidence shows pimobendan outperforms benazepril in dogs already in heart failure, and that comparison is sound. It does not show pimobendan outperforms placebo, and benazepril itself was never established against placebo at this stage, so the size of the true benefit is unknown. Owners were not blinded, and part of the composite endpoint was judged by the investigators.

question: ACE inhibitors before heart failure
species: dog
stage: pre
certainty: moderate
direction: noeffect
basis: 2 randomised placebo-controlled trials, 229 and 124 dogs
sources: SVEP, 2002 · VETPROOF, 2007
appraisal_in_preparation: yes
limitations: Both failed their primary endpoint · SVEP restricted to one breed · VETPROOF conclusions rest on secondary endpoints
verdict:
  Two placebo-controlled trials set out to show that ACE inhibition delays heart failure in preclinical mitral valve disease, and neither of them did so. The result should therefore be read as a reasonably firm negative rather than as an absence of evidence. VETPROOF is nonetheless cited as a positive trial on the strength of secondary endpoints that its design cannot carry. The same drug behaved the same way in the equivalent human trial.

question: Spironolactone added to standard heart-failure therapy
species: dog
stage: clin
certainty: low
direction: benefit
qualifier: on 39 events in a trial two thirds of dogs did not finish
basis: 1 randomised trial, 212 dogs, placebo-controlled and double-blind
sources: Bernay et al., 2010
appraisal_in_preparation: yes
limitations: Funded by the manufacturer, four of seven authors employed there · Around two thirds of dogs did not complete · Only 39 events in total · Composite includes a soft investigator-defined component
verdict:
  Spironolactone added to standard heart-failure therapy points to benefit, but the result rests on 39 events in a trial that around two thirds of dogs did not complete, and part of the composite endpoint was defined by the investigators. The manufacturer funded the study and employed four of the seven authors. The much larger human trials of the same drug are the stronger reason for using it.

question: Spironolactone with benazepril to delay the onset of heart failure
species: dog
stage: pre
certainty: moderate
direction: noeffect
basis: 1 randomised trial, 184 dogs, placebo-controlled
sources: DELAY, 2020
limitations: Failed its primary endpoint (p = 0.45) · Single-blinded · Funded by the manufacturer · Conclusion rests on surrogate secondary endpoints
verdict:
  DELAY, which tested spironolactone with benazepril before heart failure, failed its primary endpoint, and time to heart failure was numerically worse in the treated dogs. The authors state this plainly; the abstract then rests its conclusion on echocardiographic and NT-proBNP changes, which are surrogates. Read as it was designed, this is evidence that the combination does not delay the onset of heart failure.
---

question: Furosemide dose and dosing frequency in stage C disease
species: dog
stage: clin
certainty: none
direction: untested
basis: No studies
limitations: Practice rests on convention
verdict:
  No study has compared doses or dosing intervals of furosemide in dogs with heart failure. Practice derives from convention and from what worked in the individual animal. This is among the most frequently made decisions in cardiology and among the least examined, although the same gap exists in human medicine, so it is not a failing peculiar to this field.

== Dilated cardiomyopathy (dogs)

question: Pimobendan in preclinical dilated cardiomyopathy (Dobermanns)
species: dog
stage: pre
certainty: low
direction: benefit
qualifier: on time to event, not on the primary endpoint as originally analysed; Dobermanns only
basis: 1 randomised trial, 76 dogs, placebo-controlled and double-blind
sources: PROTECT, 2012
appraisal_in_preparation: yes
limitations: Primary endpoint not met by proportion (p = 0.1) · Interim analysis was not pre-planned · 76 dogs, from around 1000 screened · One breed only · Funded by the manufacturer
verdict:
  PROTECT, the preclinical pimobendan trial in Dobermanns, is routinely cited as positive. The proportion of dogs reaching its primary endpoint did not differ; the significant result comes from a time-to-event analysis at an interim look that was not pre-planned. Seventy-six Dobermanns were enrolled from approximately a thousand screened, so the finding is narrow as well as fragile, and it applies to a single breed.
---

question: Diet change and taurine in diet-associated dilated cardiomyopathy
species: dog
stage: clin
certainty: verylow
direction: unclear
qualifier: the improvement cannot be separated from the heart drugs started at the same time
basis: 1 retrospective review and 2 uncontrolled prospective cohorts, 24 to 71 dogs
sources: Kaplan, 2018 · Freid, 2021 · Freeman, 2022
appraisal_in_preparation: yes
limitations: No dog anywhere kept its original diet · No randomisation, no blinding · Echocardiograms read unblinded, by the authors' own admission · Taurine given alongside the diet change · Cardiac medication started at the same time · Cases recruited during an FDA publicity campaign · The 2022 prospective study was funded by a pet food manufacturer
verdict:
  In the diet-associated dilated cardiomyopathy literature, every dog in every study changed diet, so no comparison group exists. Improvement is confounded with the pimobendan and furosemide started at the same time, with taurine supplementation, and with regression to the mean in animals enrolled at their worst measurement. The echocardiograms were read unblinded, by the authors' own account. The one study without industry funding is also the one that states plainly that causation cannot be shown.

question: Screening intervals in at-risk breeds
species: dog
stage: pre
certainty: none
direction: untested
basis: No studies
limitations: Expert opinion only
verdict:
  No study has compared screening intervals against any outcome. The intervals in current guidelines are expert judgement, offered as such by their authors and cited afterwards as though established. Whether annual screening detects disease earlier than biennial screening, and whether earlier detection alters the outcome for the dog, both remain unanswered.

== Feline cardiomyopathy

question: Clopidogrel to prevent recurrent arterial thromboembolism
species: cat
stage: clin
certainty: moderate
direction: benefit
qualifier: compared with aspirin, in cats that had already thrown one clot
basis: 1 randomised trial, 75 cats, double-blind, active comparator, clinical endpoint
sources: FAT CAT, 2015
appraisal_in_preparation: yes
limitations: Independently funded · Active comparator, no placebo · 75 cats, wide confidence intervals · Published in a supplement issue
verdict:
  Clopidogrel after arterial thromboembolism carries the strongest independently funded evidence on this map: a double-blind randomised trial, paid for by the Morris Animal Foundation, showing longer time to recurrence than aspirin. Seventy-five cats gives wide confidence intervals, and the comparator was aspirin rather than placebo. In human medicine this drug class is used as the fallback rather than as the preferred option, and no comparison against anticoagulation has been made in cats.
---

question: Atenolol in preclinical hypertrophic cardiomyopathy
species: cat
stage: pre
certainty: low
direction: noeffect
basis: 1 prospective observational cohort, 63 cats, not randomised or blinded
sources: Schober et al., 2013
limitations: Open label, treatment not randomised · Five-year follow-up but no control for why cats were treated
verdict:
  The atenolol evidence in preclinical hypertrophic cardiomyopathy is frequently described as a trial. It is in fact a prospective observational cohort, open-label and not randomised, so the reason a cat was treated cannot be separated from what subsequently happened to it. Within those limits it found no difference in all-cause or cardiac mortality over five years. The human evidence in non-obstructive disease points against treating.
---

question: Pimobendan in feline congestive heart failure
species: cat
stage: clin
certainty: verylow
direction: unclear
basis: Retrospective records only
limitations: Retrospective · Selection bias · No prospective study
verdict:
  Pimobendan in feline congestive heart failure rests on retrospective records alone, with no prospective study and no control group. Cats given the drug were selected by their clinicians for reasons the records do not capture. The usual justification is extrapolation from dogs, and the human trials of oral inotropes are a reason to be careful about extrapolating this particular drug class. The drug is therefore in wide use on the basis of almost no direct evidence.

question: Rapamycin (sirolimus) in subclinical hypertrophic cardiomyopathy
species: cat
stage: pre
certainty: verylow
direction: unclear
qualifier: one trial reported a wall-thickness difference, on an endpoint the FDA records as selected after exploratory analysis
surrogate: yes
basis: 1 randomised trial, 43 cats, surrogate endpoint only
sources: RAPACAT, 2023 · FDA FOI summary 141-604, 2025 · [full appraisal](review-rapamycin.html)
appraisal: review-rapamycin.html
limitations: FDA records that the endpoint was chosen after exploratory analysis; the paper does not · The report calls itself nonpivotal, exploratory and dose-determining · Alpha set at 0.10, three contrasts, no correction · Overall treatment effect not significant · No individual wall measurement significant at day 180 · Analysed per protocol · All cardiac withdrawals in the high-dose arm · Transaminase elevations in 15 of 24 cats in the laboratory safety study, with no dose-response · No safety study enrolled a cat with cardiomyopathy or used the label regimen · Funded by the sponsor
verdict:
  RAPACAT is a dose-finding study, described as such by its own authors, that is now carrying a marketing approval. Wall thickness at six months was 7.41 mm on low dose against 8.40 mm on placebo, but the overall treatment effect was not significant and alpha was set at 0.10 across three uncorrected contrasts. The FDA record states that the endpoint was selected after exploratory analysis, which the published paper does not disclose. Wall thickness is in any case a proxy for heart failure rather than a measure of it.
---

question: Any therapy that delays the onset of heart failure in hypertrophic cardiomyopathy
species: cat
stage: pre
certainty: none
direction: untested
basis: No study with a clinical endpoint
limitations: Every trial to date has measured wall thickness
verdict:
  No study with a clinical endpoint has tested whether any drug delays the onset of heart failure in feline hypertrophic cardiomyopathy. Every trial so far has measured wall thickness instead. Until a trial measures heart failure, survival or quality of life, this question should be regarded as unanswered rather than as answered in the negative, since the two are not the same thing.

question: Do glucocorticoids precipitate congestive heart failure?
species: cat
stage: pre
certainty: low
direction: against
basis: 1 case series, 3 dosing studies, 1 cohort of 1042 cats
sources: Smith, 2004 · Ployngam, 2006 · Khelik, 2019 · Dutch, 2023 · [full commentary](review-steroids-chf.html)
appraisal: review-steroids-chf.html
limitations: Original series had no control group, no denominator and no pre-treatment echocardiograms · Dosing studies are mixed: two negative, one found larger atrial and ventricular dimensions · No dosing study has enrolled a cat with cardiomyopathy · Largest cohort found no excess · Steroid exposure equal in transient thickening and true cardiomyopathy
verdict:
  The teaching that glucocorticoids precipitate congestive heart failure in cats is widespread, and the published evidence does not establish it. The experimental literature is mixed rather than negative: plasma volume expansion has been demonstrated after methylprednisolone acetate, and one study found larger atrial and ventricular dimensions after short oral courses, while two others found nothing. No cat in any of them developed heart failure, and none of them enrolled a cat with cardiomyopathy, which is the population in which the effect is supposed to occur. In the only cohort with a control group, heart failure occurred in 0.82% of treated cats against 1.90% of untreated ones.

== Congestive heart failure: management

question: Torasemide as an alternative to furosemide (dogs)
species: dog
stage: clin
certainty: low
direction: noeffect
qualifier: not much worse than furosemide over three months; superiority was not shown
basis: 1 randomised non-inferiority trial, 366 dogs, active comparator, 3 months
sources: TEST, 2017
appraisal_in_preparation: yes
limitations: Non-inferiority margin set at minus 20% · Survival composite was secondary and analysed after the fact · Two trials pooled with different dosing rules · Three-month follow-up only · Funded by the manufacturer, five of nine authors employed there
verdict:
  TEST was designed to show that torasemide is not appreciably worse than furosemide, and it showed that, against a non-inferiority margin of minus 20% chosen by the investigators. The survival hazard ratio that is widely quoted from it was a secondary endpoint, analysed after the fact and pooled across two trials that used different dosing rules. Follow-up in both trials extended to three months only.
---

question: Sacubitril–valsartan (ARNi) in congestive heart failure
species: dog
stage: clin
certainty: verylow
direction: unclear
basis: 1 retrospective cohort, 50 dogs, no control group
sources: Carlson & Stern, 2026
limitations: No control group · Cardiologist-selected cases · Dosing extrapolated from human medicine · Survival compared against expectation, not a comparator
verdict:
  The evidence for sacubitril–valsartan in dogs is fifty animals, retrospective, with no control group, cases selected by cardiologists, dosing extrapolated from human medicine, and survival compared against expectation rather than against a comparator. No design of that kind can separate a drug effect from case selection. The human evidence for this drug is among the strongest in cardiology, which is a reason to run the trial rather than to skip it.
---

question: Dietary sodium restriction
species: dog
stage: clin
certainty: none
direction: untested
basis: No studies in dogs
limitations: Extrapolated from human medicine
verdict:
  No trial in dogs has tested sodium restriction against any outcome. The practice is extrapolated from human medicine, where a randomised trial has since found no effect on death or hospitalisation. Restriction also reduces palatability in animals that are frequently eating poorly already, so the advice carries a cost even if the benefit were real.
---

question: Exercise restriction after diagnosis
species: dog
stage: clin
certainty: none
direction: untested
basis: No studies
limitations: Convention only
verdict:
  Exercise restriction is advised almost universally after a cardiac diagnosis, on no evidence in either species. Human patients with heart failure are prescribed supervised aerobic training instead, and it is safe in advanced disease. Restricting exertion in order to prevent sudden death in an arrhythmogenic cardiomyopathy is a different question from restricting it in stable heart failure, and both are equally untested.

== Arrhythmias

question: Rate control in atrial fibrillation: diltiazem with digoxin versus monotherapy
species: dog
stage: clin
certainty: low
direction: benefit
qualifier: for heart rate, which is not the same as feeling better or living longer
surrogate: yes
basis: 1 randomised crossover study, 18 dogs, surrogate endpoint
sources: Gelzer et al., 2009
limitations: 18 dogs · Two weeks per arm · Measures heart rate, not survival or signs
verdict:
  Diltiazem combined with digoxin controls ventricular rate in atrial fibrillation better than either drug given alone, and that much is demonstrated in eighteen dogs studied for two weeks per arm. Whether controlling rate in this way alters clinical signs, survival, or anything an owner would notice has not been studied, because the endpoint used here is a measurement rather than an outcome.
---

question: Sotalol versus mexiletine–atenolol in Boxer arrhythmogenic cardiomyopathy
species: dog
stage: clin
certainty: low
direction: benefit
qualifier: for ectopy counts; syncope did not differ between the two effective arms
surrogate: yes
basis: 1 randomised four-arm study, 49 Boxers, surrogate endpoint
sources: Meurs et al., 2002
limitations: Around 11 to 16 dogs per arm · 21 to 28 days · Measures VPC counts · Syncope did not differ between the two effective arms
verdict:
  Sotalol and mexiletine–atenolol both reduce ventricular ectopy in Boxers, across four arms and forty-nine dogs over three to four weeks. Whether either reduces sudden death is untested, and syncope did not differ between the two effective arms. Suppression of ventricular ectopy is the intervention that increased mortality in the human trials, which is why the distinction between the count and the outcome matters here.
---

question: When to treat ventricular arrhythmias in asymptomatic dogs
species: dog
stage: pre
certainty: none
direction: untested
basis: No outcome data
limitations: No study links treatment to survival
verdict:
  No study links antiarrhythmic treatment of asymptomatic ventricular ectopy in dogs to survival, in either direction. The same question was asked directly in human medicine and answered against treatment: mortality rose while the Holter recordings improved. Practice rests on the assumption that fewer ectopic beats is better, and that is precisely the assumption which failed when it was tested in people.

question: Holter thresholds that predict sudden death
species: dog
stage: pre
certainty: low
direction: unclear
qualifier: the same dog can fall either side of a cutoff depending on how long it was recorded
basis: Screening cohorts, 28 Dobermanns on seven-day monitoring
sources: Wess et al., 2017 · Gunasekaran et al., 2020
limitations: Breed cutoffs are screening conventions · No prospective outcome validation found · Positivity depends on how long you record
verdict:
  The Holter cutoffs used to predict sudden death are screening conventions rather than thresholds validated prospectively against outcome. Whether a dog falls above or below one depends partly on how long it was recorded: six of eleven positive Dobermanns in a seven-day study were identified only after the first day. The same animal can therefore be classified either way by the same test, depending only on the recording duration.
